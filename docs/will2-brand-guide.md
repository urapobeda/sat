# WILL2 Brand Guide

WILL2 is an online education brand for SAT, IELTS, English, and international education prep.

## Core Positioning

- SAT preparation
- IELTS preparation
- English lessons
- Individual, group, and trial lessons
- Online learning worldwide
- Education-abroad guidance and resources

## Audience

Primary audience: students and young adults preparing for international exams and study abroad.

Secondary audience: parents who need the product to feel trustworthy and professional.

## Visual Direction

The product should feel like editorial education, a modern student notebook, and international academia.

Do not make it feel like a generic blue EdTech SaaS dashboard.

## Color System

- Paper background: `#f5f1e9`
- Coral brand red: `#eb6464`
- Academic blue: `#6c9ef8`
- Graphite text: `#202020`

Use bright electric colors only for limited campaign moments, not as the primary interface language.

## Typography

Use display serif typography for major headlines and clean sans-serif typography for UI, navigation, labels, body text, and controls.

Large headings may mix black, coral, and blue words.

## Graphic Language

Use sparingly:

- hand-drawn lines
- circles and underlines
- notebook/grid texture
- paper/collage details
- small annotations

Aim for 80% clean editorial layout and 20% handmade detail.

## UX Principle

Translate WILL2's visual DNA into a premium, usable web product. Do not literally recreate Instagram posts.
